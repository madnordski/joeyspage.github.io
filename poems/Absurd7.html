Absurd 7

I rode my bike today, It was a joy.

My body felt solid, Even strong on the hills.

New bests were set in spite of wind, Hills visited over decades fell under wheel.

Seven decades of living does take a toll, Just when you seem great, Your body says no.

I rode my bike today, And it felt so good.

After the ride, rested and showered. One sharp inhale, Made my back just go out!

So I sit here typing, And hoping that rest, Will make it all better, So I can do it again.

— Joey King
