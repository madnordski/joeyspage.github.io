Absurd 7

I rode my bike today, It was a joy. My body felt solid, Even strong on the hills. New bests were set with wind in my face, Hills visited over decades fell under wheel. Seven decades of living does take a toll, Just when you seem great, Your body says no. I rode my bike today, And it felt so good. After the ride, rested and showered. One sharp inhale, Brought on a back spasm. So I sit here typing, And hoping that rest, Will make it all better, ... So I can do it again.
— Joey King
